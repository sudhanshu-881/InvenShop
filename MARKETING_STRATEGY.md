# InvenShop Marketing Launch Strategy

## 🎯 **Marketing Objectives**

### Primary Goals
- **User Acquisition**: 10,000+ active shops within 6 months
- **Market Penetration**: 5% of target market in Tier 2/3 cities
- **Brand Awareness**: 80% recognition among local retailers
- **Revenue Growth**: 25% increase in shop revenue through digital tools

### Success Metrics
- **Downloads**: 50,000+ app downloads in first 3 months
- **DAU/MAU Ratio**: 80% daily active users
- **Retention**: 70% Day 7 retention, 40% Day 30 retention
- **NPS Score**: >50 (Net Promoter Score)
- **App Store Rating**: >4.5 stars
